title=PhunServer Tools
description=A collection of must have tools for running a phun server [B41/B42].
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Mod Watch[/b]. It is no phun being unable to join a server because their mods are out of date. We will help keep your apocolypse open for business by ensuring your server is always up to date!
description=[*][b]Players on map[/b]. Allow players to see one another on the mini and not so mini map. Faction only viewing is a wip
description=[*][b]Greetings[/b]. Welcome new and returning players. Say farewell to those leaving.
description=[*][b]Day/Night lengths[/b]. Set different speeds for day and night. eg 2 hour day lengths and 1 hour nights!
description=[*][b]Wipe map[/b]. Automatically wipe players maps when they relog! Designed for servers running multiple seasons that want a proper reset of their players world view.
description=[*][b]Commands[/b]. Enable the /players command so others can see who is currently online or who has been online today
description=[/list]
description=

description=[h2]PhunServer: Misc[/h2]
description=Enables server to instigate reboot if one or more mods have been recently updated. Heavily inspired by the awesome [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2844315442]Udderly Up To Date[/url] that I otherwise could not live without.
description=One important point to note is that while we can trigger a server shutdown, you will still require a host, a cron or other script/service to start it back up again. Once the server comes back up, it will automatically update mods. Many hosts (eg [url=https://www.g-portal.com]g-portal[/url]) works perfectly with this process
description=Related commands:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]/players[/b] shows all online players. Will optionally show who is recently offline
description=[/list]
description=
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Setting[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Players[/b][/td][td]Allow use of /players command in chat to display online players[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Include recent Players[/b][/td][td]When using /players, will also display those that have been online over the past 24 hours[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Debug[/b][/td][td]Print more verbose debug info in console and logs[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=

description=[h2]PhunServer: Chat[/h2]
description=Ensure your players feel seen!
description=
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Setting[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Welcome Players[/b][/td][td]Tick to send notice to all users that a player has joined (or returned)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Player Goodbye[/b][/td][td]Tick to send notice to all users that a player has logged off.[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Replace img[/b][/td][td]Tick to replace instances of [image=music] tags in text (from music) with music notes. Its prettier and cleaner. If you like uglier and dirtier, untick this box[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Replace radio[/b][/td][td]Tick this to replace the prefix Radio(station#): with a speaker icon. Because... it looks cleaner. Though there is less info (the source). Up to you[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=

description=[h2]PhunServer: ModWatch[/h2]
description=Enables server to instigate reboot if one or more mods have been recently updated. Heavily inspired by the awesome [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2844315442]Udderly Up To Date[/url] that I otherwise could not live without.
description=One important point to note is that while we can trigger a server shutdown, you will still require a host, a cron or other script/service to start it back up again. Once the server comes back up, it will automatically update mods. Many hosts (eg [url=https://www.g-portal.com]g-portal[/url]) works perfectly with this process
description=Related commands:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]/check[/b] triggers checking the workshop for updated mods. If any are found, this will start a countdown and shutdown
description=[*][b]/restart x[/b] where x is the number of minutes to countdownm, 0 for an immediate shutdown or leave x empty to trigger a restart based on your [b]Countdown[/b] settings below
description=[/list]
description=
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Setting[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Enable[/b][/td][td]When ticked, will poll Steam for updates to your servers mods and insigate a reboot if any are found. Untick to disable [/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Mod Update Notice[/b][/td][td]When a restart is triggered, give this many minutes of notice before restart[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Quit Delay[/b][/td][td]Number of seconds after saving before server quits[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Check for updates[/b][/td][td]Number of minutes between checking the workshop for mod updates[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Countdown[/b][/td][td]A semi-colon separated value of seconds of when announcements to players will be made. eg 10;20;30 would give notice when there was 30 seconds, then 20 seconds, then at 10 seconds[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Chime[/b][/td][td]Tick this box to play a little tinkle when notice is sent[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=

description=[h2]PhunServer: Maps[/h2]
description=Settings that allow players to see each other on their maps as well as options that can wipe your players map when you reset
description=
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Setting[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Players on Map[/b][/td][td]Allow players to see each other on their map[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Players on Mini Map[/b][/td][td]Allow players to see each other in their mini map[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Enable Map Wipes[/b][/td][td]Untick to prevent the players map from getting wiped[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Wipe Map[/b][/td][td]Untick if you just want to wipe symbols[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Wipe symbols[/b][/td][td]Untick if you just want to wipe the map and not the symbols[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Wipe Per Character[/b][/td][td]Tick this to trigger a map wipe when a new character is created (eg the player died)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Wipe Key[/b][/td][td]Triggers a wipe if there is a value here and it is different to the players local version. eg If you start a new season, enter Season X here. Next time the player logs in, it will wipe their map[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=

description=[h2]PhunServer: Cycles[/h2]
description=Settings that allow you to contol the start/end and speed of the Day/Night cycles
description=
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Setting[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Enable DayNight Changes[/b][/td][td]Untick to prevent PhunServer making changes to the speed of your day/night cycle[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Night Speed[/b][/td][td]Time it takes for a night to pass[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Dusk offset[/b][/td][td]Allows a +/- adjustment from the games current dusk time. eg -1 will make night start 1 hour before Dusk[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Day Speed[/b][/td][td]Time it takes for a day to pass[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Dawn offset[/b][/td][td]Allows a +/- adjustment from the games current dawn time. eg 1 will make dawn start 1 hour later[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
